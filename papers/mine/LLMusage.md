LLMs were used to implement the data processing code and scaffold the SAE training classes (a human completed and reviewed the code).  LLMs wrote many bash scripts to orchestrate experiments, especially sweeps on multiple GPUs. Humans ran the bash scripts and verified all outputs of these experiments. LLMs were used for the first draft of the paper and finding bibtex citations. Another LLM with a websearch tool and humans were used to verify citations. Humans wrote and reviewed the final draft of the paper. LLMs were also greatly helpful for proofreading grammatical errors and explaining unfamiliar concepts in statistics/biology.
